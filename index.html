Your boarding pass on your phone in three steps

No airline app to install for every trip. Check in as usual, then keep all your passes in one place.

  1. Check in with your airline

    Online, in the airline's app or at the desk. You get your boarding pass as a PDF, a mobile pass or on paper.

  2. Add it to Flight Pass

    Share the PDF or screenshot from your email, open the .pkpass file, or take a photo of the paper pass. Flight Pass reads the barcode and fills in the flight.

  3. Show it at the gate

    Before departure, a notification brings your terminal and gate. Tap it and the barcode opens full screen, at full brightness.

Questions

Does Flight Pass work with my airline?

Yes, if your airline gives you a boarding pass with a barcode. Flight Pass reads the standard barcode printed on mobile, PDF and paper boarding passes, so it works with full-service and low-cost airlines alike.

How do I open a .pkpass boarding pass on Android?

Open or share the .pkpass file with Flight Pass. It imports the pass with its barcode, and also the boarding time, terminal, gate and gate closing time when the airline includes them.

Does it work offline at the airport?

Yes. Your boarding passes are stored on your phone, so the barcode opens with no signal. Live flight status and weather need a connection.

Is Flight Pass free?

Yes. The free version shows ads, and a one-time purchase on Google Play removes them.

Do I need an account?

No account and no sign-up. Backing up your passes to your own Google Drive is optional.
